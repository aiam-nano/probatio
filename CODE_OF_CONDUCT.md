# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, criticise work rather than people, and assume good faith. Harassment, personal attacks and the publication of others' private information are not acceptable. This project adds one rule of its own: **do not post anyone's unpublished manuscript, or text from it, without their permission.**

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub (the repository's Security tab, "Report a vulnerability", also reaches the maintainer privately). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled in confidence. The maintainer may remove content and restrict participation in response.
